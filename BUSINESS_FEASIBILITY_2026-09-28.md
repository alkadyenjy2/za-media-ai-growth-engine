# Business Feasibility Audit — 2026-09-28

## Gate
First 6 months: zero-cost/free-first. Paid subscriptions only after revenue or a verified production bottleneck.

## Product thesis
AI growth engine for lead intelligence: intake → website/social intelligence → qualification → opportunity → service matching → personalization → outreach draft → evidence.

## Market evidence
Grand View Research estimates social media management at $36.4B in 2026, growing at 24.8% CAGR to 2033; SMEs were the largest organization-size segment in 2025. Source: https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/social-media-management-market-report

## Customer / GTM
Initial ICP: small agencies and businesses that need lead qualification and social intelligence without a full marketing team. Zero-cost GTM: founder-led audits, free lead intelligence samples, organic outreach, and agency partnerships.

## Monetization hypothesis
Paid managed lead-intelligence/qualification service first; SaaS subscription only after repeatable usage and verified Meta/provider integrations.

## Economics / timing
Convex backend can remain free-first while validating. External Meta/API usage and outreach scale can create variable costs. Do not pay for infrastructure until a real customer or verified bottleneck exists.

## Decision gate
BUILD/VALIDATE: visible app is viable; Meta credentials remain a real production dependency.

## Builder contract
Convex ONLY. Preserve fail-closed Meta behavior, HMAC verification, evidence qualification and no-fake-prospect rules.
